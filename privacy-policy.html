Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Veloura respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you visit our website or contact us.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your email address when you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us. We also collect limited technical information, such as browser and device details, to keep the site secure and improve its performance.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide requested communications, respond to questions, improve our products and website, and protect the service from misuse. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and Local Storage

Veloura may use browser storage to remember preferences such as your theme, cart, and wishlist. These features help the site work as expected and are stored on your device.

Third-Party Services

Some website features, such as hosted fonts, social links, or product imagery, may involve third-party services. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by contacting us. You can also clear browser storage through your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@veloura.com.
